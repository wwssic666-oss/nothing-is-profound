THE PREMIERE — MATERIALS AND WEB SCENE

Auditorium architecture, seats, draped curtains, ornament, projector, director's
chair, clothing and scene construction were created for this exhibition.

The fictional director's anatomical head and hands use the MakeHuman hm08 base
mesh with a blend of male age / proportion targets. These graphical assets
were explicitly released under CC0 in September 2020.

Source repository:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
Base mesh:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
Current asset licence:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md

The figure is a fictional character assembled from a generic anatomical mesh,
not a likeness or scan of a named filmmaker.

No colour textures, photography, or generative image backgrounds are used in
the clay renders. The architectural detail is physical scene geometry.

PBR textures were procedurally authored for this exhibition. Three.js is MIT; see vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Cormorant Garamond uses OFL; see vendor/FONT-LICENSE.txt. Sound recordings were supplied by the user.
